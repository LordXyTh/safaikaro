Hob cleaning and hood cleaning are different scopes

A hob or range is where cooking happens. A hood is the extraction canopy above it. Hood canopies and removable filters need a separate competent assessment.

Internal ducts, inaccessible fans, grease traps, waste handling, repairs, gas and electrical work, and specialist dismantling sit outside the standard cooking-line scope.

Questions before you book

Do you clean restaurant hobs and commercial ranges?

Send photos of the hobs, ranges and accessible cooking-line surfaces. SafaiKaro confirms the asset list, grease condition, suitable provider and availability before quoting. A request is not a confirmed cleaning appointment.

What is included in commercial kitchen cleaning?

The written quote names each accessible asset and surface: for example hob and range exteriors, splashbacks, counters and agreed nearby surfaces. It also states preparation, access, the working window and the agreed finish. Kitchen floor area alone does not define the work.

Is kitchen hood or filter cleaning included?

Hobs are cooking surfaces; hoods are the extraction canopies above them. Hood canopies and removable filters require a separate competent assessment and written scope. They are not automatically included in cooking-line degreasing.

Do you clean inside exhaust ducts or grease traps?

No. Internal exhaust ducts, inaccessible fans, grease traps, liquid-waste handling, gas and electrical work, repairs and specialist dismantling are outside the standard cooking-line cleaning enquiry.

Can you quote kitchen cleaning from photos?

Photos and an equipment list can help us assess accessible-surface work. Where the condition or access is unclear, a site assessment is needed. The written quotation and provider availability must be confirmed before booking.

When can the kitchen reopen after cleaning?

The provider confirms preparation, equipment cooling, utilities, the shutdown window and handover checks for the agreed work. The manager checks the completed surfaces before returning the kitchen to use. We do not publish one reopening time for every job.

Does a kitchen cleaning quote include pest treatment?

No. Cleaning and pest treatment are separately scoped and priced. If cockroaches are present, request restaurant pest control as well. SafaiKaro coordinates timing only after the service leads confirm compatible sequencing.
