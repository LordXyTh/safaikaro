Restaurant pest control questions

What does restaurant pest control in Karachi include?

SafaiKaro surveys your restaurant, records the pest activity and affected zones, and prepares a written treatment quotation. Kitchens, dry stores, drain areas, dining areas and waste points are considered according to your site. The treatment method, preparation, access window and follow-up are agreed before booking.

Is restaurant fumigation the same as cockroach treatment?

Restaurant fumigation is a common search term, but the treatment needed depends on the pest and where it is active. A cockroach problem does not automatically require treating every area with the same method. SafaiKaro confirms the method and covered zones after assessing your restaurant.

Can you treat a restaurant outside service hours?

Tell us when the kitchen and affected rooms can be out of use. SafaiKaro agrees the working window, preparation and return-to-use instructions for the proposed treatment before booking. After-hours access depends on the site and the available schedule.

What does the 90-day re-treatment guarantee cover?

SafaiKaro provides a 90-day re-treatment guarantee for pest treatment work. If activity returns, contact us for follow-up on the treated work. The written quotation identifies the pest and treated zones. A paid monthly care plan is separate from the included re-treatment promise.

Does pest treatment include hob or kitchen cleaning?

Pest treatment and cleaning are separate services. Hob degreasing, accessible cooking equipment surfaces and kitchen cleaning can be discussed alongside treatment, with their own scope and quotation. Hood filters need assessment, and internal exhaust duct cleaning is excluded.

Do you offer monthly restaurant pest control?

Yes. SafaiKaro can quote recurring restaurant pest care separately from initial treatment. The agreement sets out visit frequency, covered pests and zones, monitoring and what is charged separately. Ask for a proposed schedule based on your restaurant rather than assuming monthly care is included in a one-off visit.

Do you issue a fumigation certificate for restaurants?

Yes. SafaiKaro provides a dated certificate after commercial pest treatment, recording the work completed. Share any required format or purchasing conditions before booking so they can be checked. A treatment certificate does not promise regulatory approval, a passed inspection or a food-safety certification.

How much does restaurant fumigation cost in Karachi?

Restaurant pest control is quoted from the pest activity, kitchen and floor layout, affected zones, access and working window. Send the broad area, approximate restaurant size and what you have seen. SafaiKaro confirms the survey next step and provides a written quote before work starts.
