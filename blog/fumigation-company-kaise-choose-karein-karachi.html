Karachi Mein Fumigation Company Kaise Choose Karein

SafaiKaro Team 5 min read
Fumigation Checklist Karachi

Before you book a fumigation company in Karachi, ask seven things: is the price given before work and in writing, which pests and areas are covered, what happens at the visit, how long you stay out, what happens if pests return, who does the work, and how the deposit works. Clear answers to all seven are a good sign.

Agar pehle koi company baat kar ke mukar gayi, ya spray ke 2 din baad wohi haal tha, to aap ka shak theek hai. Fumigation mein aksar masla spray ka nahi hota. Masla yeh hota hai ke baat pehle saaf nahi hui. Yeh 7 sawal booking se pehle WhatsApp par puch lein, kisi bhi company se.

Key takeaways

  • Get the price before the visit, in writing.
  • Ask the company to name your pest, your area and what the visit includes.
  • Get re-treatment terms and deposit terms in writing too.
  • Ask who does the work, not who answers the phone.

1. Price Pehle Milti Hai, Aur Likh Kar?

A fair quote is a number you have before anyone enters the house. It depends on property size, so a company should ask for your square yards or number of rooms, then give one price. A WhatsApp message counts as writing.

Price changes on the day are where most arguments start. SafaiKaro publishes its prices on the pest control price list and confirms the price before work starts. Compare any quote against a published list like that.

2. Kaun Se Pest Aur Kaun Se Area Cover Hote Hain?

"Fumigation" means different jobs for different pests. General fumigation in Karachi is usually a residual spray for cockroaches, ants and other crawling insects. Termites are a separate job: the colony lives in the soil, so the treatment is drill-and-inject. Bed bugs need two sessions.

A company that quotes one price for everything has not looked at your problem.

Ask it to name your pest and your area. Our pages for termite treatment and bed bug treatment show how those jobs differ from a general spray.

3. Visit Par Kya Kaam Hota Hai?

Ask the company to describe the visit in steps. A good answer covers inspection, which areas are treated (kitchen, drains, skirting, cabinets), what is applied, and who confirms the size tier and price on the day. Ask whether chemicals, equipment and labour are included in the quote.

Ask what "fumigation" means for them. Some people expect gas in a sealed house. The standard home service in Karachi is a spray, and you can go back in after it dries. Our fumigation services in Karachi page lists what a visit includes and how it runs.

4. Ghar Khali Karna Parega? Kitni Der?

Ask for an exact time, not "thori der". For a standard SafaiKaro fumigation, children and pets stay out of treated rooms and you can go back in after 30 minutes to 1 hour, once the surfaces are dry. Your technician confirms the timing on the day.

Also ask what to prepare. Usually you cover or move food, clear the space under the kitchen sink and move furniture off the walls. If you have infants, elderly family members or pets, tell the company when you book. Our guide on ghar ko fumigation ke liye tayar karna has the full checklist.

5. Pest Wapas Aaye To Kya, Aur Kya Likha Hai?

This is the question people forget, and the one that matters most after a bad experience. Ask three things: how many days the company will come back, what counts as "pests returned", and what is not covered. Ask for it in writing.

SafaiKaro's term is simple: if pests return within 90 days, we re-treat at no additional cost. For termites, repair of eaten wood and tiles is not included, because that is carpenter and mason work. Whatever a company promises, the exclusions should be written next to the promise.

6. Kaam Kaun Karta Hai?

The person who answers the phone is often not the person who arrives. Ask whether the company's own team does the job or whether it is passed to someone else. Our own team does the work.

7. Payment Kaise Hoti Hai, Deposit Wapas Milta Hai?

Ask what the booking deposit is, whether it is refundable and when. Ask for a receipt.

At SafaiKaro, the deposit is Rs 500 and refundable: if you cancel 24 hours before your appointment it is refunded in full. Later than that it is not, because the technician is already allocated. The balance is paid on the day, by Easypaisa, JazzCash, bank transfer or cash.

A company that asks for a large advance, with no receipt and no refund terms, is asking you to trust it before it has done anything. Book someone who answers all seven questions. When you are ready, you can book a slot with us or ask us the seven first.
