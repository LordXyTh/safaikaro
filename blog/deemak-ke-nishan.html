Deemak Insect Hota Kaisa Hai?

Deemak insect chhota, naram, halke rang ka keera hai jo zameen ke andar colony banata hai. Aap ko woh shayad hi nazar aaye. Nazar aate hain uske nishan.

Karachi ke gharon mein jo deemak milti hai woh zameen se upar aati hai: slab ki daraar, expansion joint ya pipe ke suraakh se. Is liye ghar ke andar nishan dikhna matlab colony neeche pehle se hai. Neeche 6 cheezein hain, ek ek karke check kar lein.

1. Deewar Par Mitti Ki Naali (Mud Tube)

Skirting ke saath, bathroom ki deewar par ya pipe ke paas pencil jitni moti mitti ki lakeer. Yeh deemak ka raasta hai: neeche colony se upar lakdi tak. Sab se pehle bathroom, kitchen sink ke neeche aur AC drain ke paas dekhein, kyunki wahan nami bhi hoti hai.

Mitti ka dhabba samajh kar poncha maar diya? Kuch din baad wahi lakeer wapas aa jati hai. Yehi sign hai.

Pehle photo le lein, phir ek tukra tod kar dekhein. Andar taza geeli mitti aur chhote safed keere hon to deemak abhi active hai.

2. Tap Karne Par Khokhli Lakdi

Darwaze ki chaukhat, khidki ka frame ya almari par ungli ke pore ya screwdriver ke dastay se halke se tap karein. Awaaz thethri ya khokhli aaye to lakdi andar se kha li gayi hai. Upar se woh bilkul theek lagti hai, isi liye log der se pakadte hain.

Chaukhat ko neeche se, farsh ke paas se tap karna shuru karein. Deemak zameen se chadhti hai, to neechla hissa sab se pehle jaata hai.

3. Chaukhat Ya Almari Mein Tunnel

Agar lakdi ka koi tukra toot jaye ya chhil jaye to andar lambi lambi naaliyan aur mitti nazar aati hai. Upar ki parat patli reh jaati hai, andar ka hissa khaali. Almari ka woh pichla hissa jo deewar se lagta hai, aur kitchen cabinets ka neechla khaana, yeh dono jagah zaroor dekhein.

Torch se dekhein, lakdi ko tod kar mat dekhein. Tooti hui chaukhat ki photo hi kaafi hai.

4. Khidki Ke Paas Pankh

Baarish ke baad khidki ke sill par, light ke neeche ya fan ke paas chhote chhote pankh bikhre milte hain. Udne wali deemak (swarmer) nayi jagah dhoondti hai aur pankh chhod deti hai. Mahine ba mahine ka hisaab deemak ka mausam Karachi mein post mein hai.

Kahin yeh udne wali cheenti to nahi? Farq pankh mein hai. Deemak ke chaaron pankh barabar lambe hote hain, cheenti ke aage ke pankh peeche walon se bade hote hain. Pankh ghar ke andar mile to pehle photo lein, phir jhaad dein.

5. Almari Ya Shelf Ke Neeche Bareek Burada

Wooden almari ya shelf ke neeche sawdust jaisa burada, ya chhote chhote dane. Yeh zyada tar lakdi mein rehne wali (drywood) deemak ka nishan hai. Karachi ke gharon mein aam tor par zameen wali deemak milti hai, is liye yeh nishan kam milta hai aur har burada deemak ka nahi hota. Kisi aur keere ka bhi ho sakta hai.

Is liye sirf burada dekh kar faisla na karein. Baqi 5 cheezein bhi check karein, aur burada ki photo saath bhej dein.

6. Paint Ka Bubble, Bina Leakage Ke

Deewar ya ceiling ka paint phool jaye ya woh jagah narm lage, aur paani ki leakage na ho, to andar deemak ho sakti hai. Wooden ceiling boarding wale ghar mein yeh zyada nazar aata hai.

Pehle yeh check karein ke upar ki tanki, bathroom ya pipe se seelan to nahi aa rahi. Seelan ho to paint wahi karega. Seelan na ho aur baqi nishanon mein se koi mil jaye, tab deemak ka shak barh jaata hai.
