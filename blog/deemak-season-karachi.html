Deemak kab aati hai Karachi mein? Season calendar

Deemak kab aati hai, is ka seedha jawab: woh kabhi jaati nahi. Karachi ki deemak subterranean hai, colony zameen mein hai, aur Karachi ki zameen itni thandi nahi hoti ke colony so jaaye. Jo badalta hai woh nami hai:

  • July se September (monsoon): Baarish zameen ko geela karti hai, colony sab se tez barhti hai aur sab se zyada lakdi khaati hai. Nuqsan deewar ke andar hota hai, nazar nahi aata.
  • September ke aakhir se October: Aakhri baarishon ke baad shaam ko par wali deemak nikalti hai, pankh khidki ke paas girte hain, mitti ki naaliyan taaza hoti hain. SafaiKaro ki termite calls is mahine se barhti hain.
  • October se November: Din 30 degree tak, nami October mein 54 se 62 percent, November mein 44 se 51. Zameen abhi geeli hai, colony foundation ke paas khaati hai. Inspection ka sab se behtar waqt.
  • December se February (sardi): Karachi ki sardi halki hai. Bahar ki harkat dheemi hoti hai, lekin almari ke peeche aur bathroom ki geeli deewar par colony khaati rehti hai. December ya January ki ek baarish chhota swarm dobara nikaal sakti hai.

Khulasa: nuqsan monsoon mein, pehchaan October mein. Agar colony hai aur October mein pakdi nahi gayi, toh woh agle monsoon tak bina roke khaati hai.

Dimak kyu lagti hai: nami, seepage aur Karachi ki zameen

Deemak lagna teen cheezon ka natija hai: geeli mitti, lakdi, aur dono ke beech raasta. Pakistan ki ghar wali species, Coptotermes, Odontotermes aur Heterotermes, mitti se nami leti hain aur mitti ki naali (mud tube) bana kar lakdi tak pohnchti hain. Nami khatam toh naali sookh jaati hai. Har wajah ke peeche paani hai.

Samandar aur reclaimed zameen. Clifton ke samandar wale blocks aur DHA Phase 8 ka bara hissa neechi ya reclaimed zameen par hai: water table upar, mitti silty, der tak geeli. Phase 5, 6 aur 7 ke bungalows mein lawn saal bhar paani leta hai aur boundary wall ke saath lakdi ka gate ya pergola seedha mitti ko chhoota hai. Is liye October mein hamari zyada tar deemak visits DHA aur Clifton mein hoti hain.

Seepage. Sab se aam raasta bathroom ki deewar hai. Tile joints se paani deewar mein jaata hai, saath wali almari ya chaukhat geeli rehti hai, aur deemak ko bahar jaane ki zaroorat nahi rehti. Purani PECHS aur Clifton Block 2 se 4 ki buildings mein leaking pipes yahi kaam karti hain.

Flats vs bungalows. Bungalow mein deemak chaaron taraf se aa sakti hai: lawn, boundary wall, porch ka joint. Flat mein raasta plumbing ducts, expansion joints aur deewar ki daraarein hain, aur upar wale flat ka seepage aap ki deewar geeli rakhta hai. Ground floor aur basement store pehle, baqi floors baad mein.

Lakdi. Darwaze, chaukhat, built-in almari aur kitchen cabinets lakdi hain. Deewar mein chuni hui purani chaukhat sab se aasaan target hai: ek taraf geeli deewar, doosri taraf khaana.

Par wali deemak: baarish ke baad udne wali deemak ka matlab

Baarish ke baad umas wali shaam ko jo chhote bhoore keeray roshni ke gird udte hain aur phir pankh chhod dete hain, woh deemak ke swarmers (alates) hain: colony ke nar aur maada jo nayi colony banane nikle hain. Mature colony inhein baarish ke baad nikaalti hai; geeli mitti signal hai ke zameen mein ghusna aasaan hai.

Udne wali deemak khud nuqsan nahi karti. Woh ek ghante se kam udti hai, phir pankh jhaad deti hai; jo jodi bana leti hai woh geeli mitti mein ghus jaati hai, baqi mar jaati hain. Asal sawal hai ke aayi kahan se. Swarm kisi chaukhat, skirting ya bathroom ki deewar se nikla toh colony aap ki building mein ya us ke neeche hai. Khidki ke raste bahar se aayi toh colony paros mein hai aur aap ki geeli deewar agla target ho sakti hai.

Us shaam: lights aur khidki band karein, pankh aur keeray jhaaru se ikattha karke photo lein. Spray mat karein, farsh par dawa phailne ke siwa kuch nahi hota. Agle din deewar aur skirting ke saath mitti ki naali dhoondein aur photo WhatsApp karein.

Deemak ki pehchaan: October mein kaun se signs pehle dikhte hain

Deemak kaisi dikhti hai? Worker safed, naram aur chaawal ke daane se chhoti hoti hai, aur roshni se bhaagti hai. Aap usay nahi, us ka kaam dekhte hain. October mein yeh signs is tarteeb se aate hain:

  1. Mitti ki naali (mud tube): deewar, skirting ya bathroom tiles ke saath pencil jitni moti bhoori naali. Ek jagah tod kar agle din dekhein: dobara ban gayi toh colony active hai.
  2. Khokhli chaukhat: darwaze ki chaukhat ko neeche se tap karein. Theek lakdi ki awaaz bhari, khaayi hui ki khaali aur kaaghaz jaisi.
  3. Pankh: khidki ki sill, light ke neeche ya jaal ke paas ek jaise size ke bahut se chhote shaffaf pankh.
  4. Paint mein bubble ya bhoori lakeer: bina leakage ke. Deemak paint ke neeche mitti chipka kar chalti hai.
  5. Almari ka back panel: deewar ke saath wali built-in almari ka panel dabaane par narm lage ya us par mitti ke daagh hon.

Ek sign bhi hai toh colony pehle se khaa rahi hai, aksar pichhle monsoon se.

Deemak se bachao: ghar par kya karein, aur DIY se kya milta hai

How to avoid deemak, is ka zyada tar jawab paani hai. Jo aap khud kar sakte hain aur jo asar karta hai:

  • Seepage band karein. Bathroom ke tile joints, kitchen sink ke neeche, AC drain, overhead tank ki line. Geeli deewar sookh jaaye toh deemak ka raasta tang ho jaata hai.
  • Lakdi ko mitti se alag karein. Lawn mein gate, bench ya spare plywood seedha mitti par na ho. Boundary wall ke saath patte jama na hone dein.
  • Furniture dhoop mein. Ek chhota piece affected hai toh 3 se 4 din tez dhoop us ki deemak maar deti hai. Yeh us almari ka hal hai, deewar ka nahi.
  • Mahine mein ek baar tap test. Chaukhat, skirting, almari ka back panel.

Ab woh jo DIY nahi karta. Dimak ka spray, kerosene ya market ki deemak medicine surface par nazar aane wale workers ko maarti hai. Colony zameen mein hai, us ke paas hazaron aur workers hain, aur kuch hafton mein wahi naali dobara ban jaati hai. Repellent spray (pyrethroid class, jaise bifenthrin) deemak ko mehsoos hota hai, woh raasta badal leti hai, aksar doosri chaukhat ki taraf. Boric acid lakdi par kuch waqt kaam karta hai, soil mein nahi jaata.

DIY ka asal faida paani aur pehchaan hai. Colony khatam karna soil treatment ka kaam hai.
