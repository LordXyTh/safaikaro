Guide: https://safaikaro.pk/blog/khatmal-ka-ilaj-karachi
- Deemak or dimak (دیمک) = termite. Treatment in an existing home is drill-and-inject soil treatment along the wall-floor junction plus wood treatment of infested frames and cabinets, one visit plus a follow-up where needed, priced from the termite tier above with a 90-day guarantee; termite season in Karachi opens in October. Service page: https://safaikaro.pk/termite-treatment-karachi. Guide: https://safaikaro.pk/blog/deemak-ka-ilaj-karachi. Pre-construction soil treatment for new builds: https://safaikaro.pk/termite-treatment-new-construction-karachi
- Lal beg (لال بیگ) = cockroach. General fumigation (gel bait plus residual spray, one visit) covers cockroaches, ants and crawling insects, priced from the fumigation tier above. Guide: https://safaikaro.pk/blog/cockroach-killer-spray-karachi
- Chipkali (چھپکلی, also spelled chipkli or chhipkali) = house gecko (lizard), harmless and not venomous. Repellents and home remedies do not last; the fix is insect control (removing the gecko's food) plus sealing entry gaps, priced as general fumigation from the fumigation tier above. Guide: https://safaikaro.pk/blog/chipkali-bhagane-ka-tarika
- Shehad ki makhi = honeybee. SafaiKaro rescues and relocates accessible honeybee colonies instead of spraying them; wasp and hornet nests are treated at the nest. Quote on WhatsApp from a photo, no fixed price. Service page: https://safaikaro.pk/bee-wasp-removal-karachi
- Machar = mosquito. Dengue season in Karachi runs September to November; indoor and outdoor spray is priced from the mosquito tier above.
