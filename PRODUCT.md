# SafaiKaro commercial services

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Restaurant owners and managers, office facilities teams and institutional buyers in Karachi seeking a written scope for pest treatment or commercial kitchen cleaning.

## Product Purpose

Help a buyer understand the service scope and request a survey or written quotation through WhatsApp or phone.

## Positioning

Karachi commercial pest control and cleaning, with site requirements and exclusions agreed before work starts.

## Conversion & proof

The primary action is a commercial quote or survey request. Calling is an alternative. An anonymous completed restaurant cockroach treatment is founder-confirmed proof. Existing AI-generated kitchen images are illustrations, not project evidence. No client names, review ratings, certifications or inspection outcomes are supplied for the new pages.

## Brand Personality

The existing website uses bold, direct, practical copy with a green identity.

## Design Principles

Preserve SafaiKaro's existing brand. Explain what the quotation covers. Keep treatment callbacks separate from paid recurring care. Describe only supplied proof and service capabilities.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Existing visible focus states and labelled controls should be preserved. New navigation and quote preparation must support keyboard and mobile use.
