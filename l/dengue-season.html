One visit, three jobs

Coils and aerosol cans kill the mosquito in front of you. They do nothing about the next batch hatching in your planter.

  • Indoor fogging of bedrooms, lounge, bathrooms and store, where adult Aedes mosquitoes rest during the day.
  • Outdoor spray along the lawn, boundary wall, shrubs and shaded corners.
  • Larvicide in drains, cooler trays, flower pots and any water you cannot empty, so the next generation does not hatch.
  • Children and pets cleared from rooms during treatment, with re-entry timing given by the technician.

Why timing matters more than the spray

Aedes mosquitoes breed in clean standing water: a bucket on the roof, a plant saucer, the tray under the AC. After every rain the count goes up within a week.

Treating early in the season, before cases appear in your block, breaks the cycle while it is still small. Waiting for the first case means the breeding has already happened.
