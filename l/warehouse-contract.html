What a warehouse visit actually includes

Godown losses are rarely one big event. It is chewed cartons, contaminated sacks and a pallet that collapses because the base was eaten through.

  • Numbered bait stations along walls and racking, checked and logged on every visit.
  • Entry points at shutters, drains and cable gaps identified and listed for your maintenance team.
  • Termite treatment for wooden pallets, door frames and any timber in contact with the floor, scoped separately.
  • A dated visit record per visit and the 90-day re-treatment guarantee on the treatment work.

Why godowns keep getting rats back

One spray does nothing for a rat problem. Rats follow routes along walls and under racking, and a building with open shutters and a drain behind it gets new arrivals every week.

The fix is a station plan: fixed points, checked on a cadence, with the activity written down so you can see whether it is falling.
