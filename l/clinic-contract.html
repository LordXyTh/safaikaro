Why clinics end up with cockroaches

Clinics have constant water use, a pantry that feeds staff all day, and waste bins that fill faster than a normal office. Cockroaches follow the drains and the bins.

Patients notice. A single sighting in the waiting area undoes a lot of trust, and it rarely gets reported to you directly.
