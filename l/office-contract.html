Where office pests actually live

In most Karachi offices the cockroaches are not on the desks. They come up through the washroom stack and the pantry drain, and they breed behind the water dispenser and under the microwave shelf.

Rats use cable trays and the false ceiling as a highway between floors. One sighting in a meeting room usually means a route that runs the length of the building.

Office mein spray karwana asaan hai, masla yeh hai ke do mahine baad wapas aa jate hain. A schedule fixes that, a one-time spray does not.
