What a planned schedule covers

Parents and members judge a school or gym by its washrooms and its canteen. Those are the zones we start with.

  • Canteen, tuck shop and storerooms treated for cockroaches, ants and rodents.
  • Changing rooms, showers and floor drains, where gym cockroach problems almost always start.
  • Courtyards, planters, water tanks and roof drains checked after the monsoon for mosquito breeding.
  • A dated visit record for your principal, owner or building committee, plus the 90-day re-treatment guarantee on the treatment work.

Two buildings, the same problem

A school and a gym look different, but the pest pressure comes from the same places: wet areas, food areas, and spaces that stay closed for days.

Schools are easiest to treat during term breaks and after dismissal. Gyms are easiest late at night or on the day the floor is closed. Either way, nobody should be inside during treatment.
